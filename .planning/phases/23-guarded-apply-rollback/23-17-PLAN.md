<objective>
Give the engine its one restore path and route every way the under-lock window can fail through it: a write failure, a lapsed lock (by the gateway or by the local clock), an incomplete scan and a red master verify restore the snapshot and scan; a changed pre-image or a competing lock refuses with nothing moved; a restore that cannot finish is `rollback_failed` with the journal kept.

Purpose: DAPL-06 and DAPL-07, the guarantee that an apply ends verified or restored and scanned, never as a partially loaded tree, proven on the local host with the fake gateway where fault injection is cheap (ROADMAP constraint; "one `restore_snapshot(run)` serves abort, auto-rollback and manual rollback"). Split from 23-05 so each plan fits the context budget: 23-05 built the green path and the hold layer. The tests pass explicit `HoldModel` values; the measured hold arrives in 23-10 (D-02) before any verb exposes the engine.
Output: `restore_snapshot`, the abort and auto-rollback branches of `run_apply`, the rollback fields of `DeployApplyResult`, `FakeGateway::stall_scans`, the fault and auto-rollback suites in `tests/deploy_apply.rs`.
</objective>
