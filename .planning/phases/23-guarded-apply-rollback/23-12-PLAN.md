<objective>
Make an interrupted apply safe: the on-box journal blocks plan, apply and rollback until `rollback --run <id>` recovers it, with the D-10 no-restore case, the D-11 staleness exemption and the D-12 manual guidance.

Purpose: DAPL-09 with D-09 to D-12. The journal is written first under the lock (23-04); this plan makes every verb respect it and gives recovery its exact rules, proven by aborting a real apply task mid-write.
Output: journal refusal in `deploy_plan` and the apply and rollback prepares, recovery in the rollback path, `recovery_action`, `journal_` tests.
</objective>
