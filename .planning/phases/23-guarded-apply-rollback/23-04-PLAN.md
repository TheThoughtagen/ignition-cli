<objective>
Define the write seam the engine drives (`ApplyHost`), implement all nine of its operations natively on the local filesystem, and open the conformance table that the POSIX and PowerShell hosts must also pass.

Purpose: D-01 moves every under-lock operation into one host call. Getting the operation order, the pre-image check, the snapshot and the restore right once, in Rust, on the cheap local host, makes the scripts a translation job held to the same table. This plan was split for context budget: 23-19 (next wave) adds the table's commit and restore rule rows and the fault seams (`ApplyFault`, `FaultyHost`) that make the host fault-injectable for the engine (23-05).
Output: `deploy/apply/host.rs`, `deploy/transport/batch.rs`, `impl ApplyHost for LocalFsTransport`, `apply_host_for_node`, `tests/deploy_host_conformance.rs` with its first seven rows.
</objective>
